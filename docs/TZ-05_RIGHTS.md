# TZ-05 publication guard

The branch includes a local SQLite-backed rights review ledger and a framework-neutral HTTP boundary. A server-verified artist or label may submit an item-and-region claim with a digest of privately stored evidence, explicit DRE-05 use grants, and a bounded license window. A separate server-verified rights reviewer may approve or deny it for a bounded time; self-review is denied. Publication fails closed unless the requested DRE-05 use, territory, license window, review window, tenant, application and item all match. Withdrawal revokes approval, timestamps cannot move backward, and every transition records an audit event without raw rights documents. Client-supplied identity or reviewer roles cannot authorize a transition.

`node --test tests/rights-registry.test.mjs` covers approval, expiry, withdrawal, granted-use, license-window, role/region isolation, monotonic audit time, UTF-8 request limits, injected write interruption and HTTP denial. The local use vocabulary and validity semantics are pinned to Distributed Radio Engine DRE-05 merged PR #6; TrackZero stores no DRE package or live engine credentials.

This is a software review workflow, not a legal opinion, licensed broadcaster, production rights registry or operational publishing path. A real identity provider, private evidence store, authenticated reviewer workflow, jurisdiction and ownership checks, takedown/dispute process, live DRE service integration, and product deployment still require integration and evidence. Missing approvals fail closed. Never infer a license from artist metadata or past uploads.
